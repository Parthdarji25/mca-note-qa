What is ethical hacking?

Ethical hacking is the authorised practice of probing a system for weaknesses so they can be fixed before attackers find them.

  • It is done with the owner's written permission.
  • Findings are reported, not exploited.
Remember: permission and scope come first.

Differentiate between white-hat, black-hat and grey-hat hackers.

TypeIntentPermission
White hatDefend and improve securityYes
